Figure 2: Example palette outputs across four representative image types.
Left column: source images with mean L\* annotated. Method columns:
five-colour palettes ordered by assigned role (Surface, On-Surface,
Primary, Secondary, Accent), with min ΔE2000 annotated below each strip.
Role assignment uses the same heuristic across all methods (mode=auto for
the dark image row); differences in role coherence reflect differences in
selected colours, not in role-assignment logic. DSP produces palettes
where role assignments yield visually distinct, design-system-coherent
outputs across all four image types; baselines often assign roles to
perceptually clustered colours, particularly visible in the colourful
image row (Surface and Primary near-indistinguishable in k-Means Lab and
RGB) and the dark image row (Surface/On-Surface contrast pair fails
WCAG AA in all three baselines).
